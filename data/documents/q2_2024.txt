Q2 2024 CFO QUARTERLY COMMENTARY

Revenue Performance:
Q2 2024 revenue of $5.61M exceeded budget by 2.3% and grew 10.8% versus
Q2 2023. Growth was balanced across Enterprise and SMB segments.
Annual contract value (ACV) per new customer increased 7% year-over-year,
reflecting the product's move upmarket and pricing strategy execution.

EBITDA and Margin:
Q2 2024 EBITDA margin of 25.2% improved 60 basis points versus Q1 2024
(24.6%). Cost discipline across Sales and Marketing drove the improvement.
Sales as a percent of revenue declined 80 basis points versus Q1 as the
team achieved higher revenue with a stable headcount base.

Hiring Update:
The Engineering hiring plan approved in Q1 is executing on schedule.
15 software engineers were added in Q2 with an additional 12 planned
for Q3. These hires are expected to compress EBITDA margins by 100-150
basis points in Q3 2024 before the productivity ramp in Q4-Q1 2025.
Management views this investment as essential to the H2 2025 product roadmap.

Balance Sheet:
Cash position remains strong at $14.2M with no debt. Monthly burn is
well within plan. Runway exceeds 24 months at current spend levels.
