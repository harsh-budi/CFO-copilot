Q3 2024 CFO QUARTERLY COMMENTARY

Revenue Performance:
Q3 2024 revenue of $5.82M came in 3.1% above budget and 11.4% above
Q3 2023. Enterprise segment drove outperformance, growing 18% year-over-year
on strong net new logo additions in July and August. SMB revenue was softer
than expected, tracking 4% below budget due to longer sales cycles.

EBITDA and Margin:
Q3 2024 EBITDA margin of 23.8% contracted 140 basis points versus Q2 2024
(25.2%) and 90 basis points versus Q3 2023 (24.7%). The primary driver was
elevated Engineering headcount costs following the Q2 hiring plan execution.
Engineering compensation expense ran $340K above budget in Q3 as new hires
ramped — representing 18% cost inflation in the department. This was a
planned investment to accelerate the product roadmap.

Gross Margin:
Gross margin remained stable at 72.1% in Q3 2024, in line with Q2 2024
and slightly above the 71.6% recorded in Q3 2023. No material change in
COGS structure — the margin compression is entirely below the gross profit line.

Key Risks and Outlook:
Marketing efficiency deteriorated in Q3 with CAC increasing 9% quarter-over-quarter.
The sales team is implementing a revised outbound targeting strategy to improve
pipeline quality in Q4. Q4 revenue pipeline coverage is at 2.4x — management
remains confident in achieving full-year guidance. G&A expense ran 6% above
budget in Q3 due to one-time legal fees related to a vendor contract dispute,
now resolved.
