Q1 2024 CFO QUARTERLY COMMENTARY

Revenue Performance:
Q1 2024 revenue of $5.19M was 1.2% below budget due to January slowness
driven by customer budget freeze patterns typical of Q1. February and March
rebounded strongly with February representing the highest monthly bookings
in company history at $1.94M ARR added. Full year guidance maintained.

EBITDA and Margin:
Q1 2024 EBITDA margin of 24.6% was in line with the prior quarter (24.8%
in Q4 2023). No material cost variances — all departments tracked within
5% of budget. G&A efficiency improved as fixed costs were absorbed across
a larger revenue base.

Annual Plan:
Full year 2024 revenue target is $67.5M, implying 10.8% growth over 2023.
EBITDA margin guidance of 24-26% for full year, with H2 margin pressure
expected from the Engineering hiring plan.

Key Focus Areas for 2024:
1. Enterprise segment expansion — target 15 new logos above $500K ACV
2. Engineering investment — 27 net new hires planned to accelerate roadmap
3. CAC efficiency — reduce blended CAC by 8% through improved targeting
4. International expansion — pilot launch in Canada and UK in Q3 2024
