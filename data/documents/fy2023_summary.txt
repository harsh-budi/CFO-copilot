FULL YEAR 2023 FINANCIAL SUMMARY

Revenue:
FY2023 total revenue of $60.9M, growing 12.3% versus FY2022 ($54.2M).
Revenue exceeded the original annual plan of $59.8M by 1.8%.
Q4 2023 was the strongest quarter at $16.2M, benefiting from year-end
enterprise deal closures and strong renewal rates.

Profitability:
FY2023 EBITDA of $15.1M representing a 24.8% margin, improving from
22.4% in FY2022. Margin expansion driven by: (1) revenue scale absorbing
fixed G&A costs, (2) improved Sales efficiency with CAC declining 11%,
(3) stable gross margins at 71.8%.

Headcount:
Total headcount ended 2023 at 187 employees, up from 156 at end of 2022.
Net adds were concentrated in Engineering (18 hires) and Sales (12 hires).
Annual attrition was 8.3%, below the industry benchmark of 12-15%.

Cash and Balance Sheet:
Year-end cash of $12.8M. The company generated $9.4M of free cash flow
in FY2023, the first year of positive FCF in company history.
No debt on the balance sheet. The company is fully self-funded.
